# Corpus Augustinianum — Index & Catalogus Operum

**Sancti Aurelii Augustini Hipponensis Opera Omnia**

This dedicated section contains **281 individual scholarly Markdown treatises** (~64.8 MB) derived from the Universität Zürich *Corpus Corporum* TEI editions and the *Corpus Scriptorum Ecclesiasticorum Latinorum* (CSEL) critical editions.

## 1. Summary of Collections

- **Genuine Augustinian Works (`Aug - `):** 203 treatises, pastoral letters, and sermons.
- **Pseudo-Augustinian Works (`Ps-Aug - `):** 45 apocryphal / unverified works historically transmitted under Augustine's name (*auctor incertus*).
- **Historical, Biographical & Reception Works (`Other - `):** 33 contemporary documents (Possidius, Prosper of Aquitaine, Orosius, Eugippius, Maurist Life).
